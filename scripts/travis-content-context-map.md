## 2. Bucket-by-Bucket Playbook

Only buckets with 10 or more videos are profiled. Each profile lists the typical structure, hook pattern, audience target, CTA approach, and when to reach for this bucket when writing new scripts.

### travis_solo_tutorial (222)

Travis on camera walking the viewer through a process step by step. The largest single bucket and the spine of the channel's tutorial library. Often opens with a tutorial promise, then a numbered walkthrough, and closes by pointing the viewer to either the free Amazon FBA course resource or the paid program.

- **Typical body structure:** step_by_step_tutorial (177), numbered_list (39)
- **Typical hook type:** tutorial_promise (147), list_promise (28), money_promise (23)
- **Audience intent:** beginner_aspiring (152), intermediate_seller (65)
- **CTA type:** multiple_layered (106), course_link (47), free_resource (47)
- **Ending move:** course_link_pitch (100), free_resource_pitch (83), subscribe_outro (19)
- **Framing style:** technical_walkthrough (128), warm_mentor (78)

Examples to study:

- `3IDes4pccHM` Amazon FBA For Beginners 2026 (Step by Step Tutorial)
- `mCqh9sV3hm8` FREE Amazon FBA Course | COMPLETE Step by Step Tutorial For Beginners
- `cFxW_MAXrIE` FREE Amazon FBA Course for Beginners $0-$100k in 7 Days
- `huykBLuIB5A` Free AMAZON PRODUCT RESEARCH Method (Beats Jungle Scout & Viral Launch 100%!)
- `_hFtKmWVmSg` How to Sell On Amazon FBA 2025 - The Passion Product Formula
- `erajlDCiQNE` How To Sell On Amazon FBA As A Beginner - Step by Step Tutorial

- `-akLRm_uBS4` CRAZY Amazon FBA Product Research Technique That Found Me A $40,000/Month Product

**Use this bucket when:** writing a step-by-step how-to (Amazon setup, Helium 10 walkthrough, product launch checklist) for a beginner audience who needs to follow along. Default framing is technical_walkthrough or warm_mentor, default ending is layered (free resource + course link).
