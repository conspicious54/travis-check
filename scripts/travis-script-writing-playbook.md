# Travis Marziani Script-Writing Playbook

A working playbook for drafting Travis-voiced YouTube scripts. Built from pattern analysis of 696 videos across his catalog. If you have a topic and an audience, you can draft a full script from this document.

---

## 1. How to use this playbook (quick start flow)

1. **Pick your video_type** from the five templates in Section 3. If the goal is to teach a process step by step, use `travis_solo_tutorial`. If the goal is to argue a thesis or list mistakes/secrets, use `travis_solo_advice` or `listicle_top_n`. If you have a real student or self-result with numbers, use `student_case_study`. If you are forcing the viewer to choose between two named products or paths, use `vs_comparison`.
2. **Run the pre-script checklist** in Section 2. Do not start writing until every answer is on the page.
3. **Match the hook** to the template. Each template lists 3-5 verbatim hooks from real Travis videos. Pick one that fits your angle and rewrite it for your topic. Keep the rhythm.
4. **Fill the body skeleton** for that template. Use the bracketed slots in order. Do not rearrange them.
5. **Close with the ending move** for that template. Pair it with a CTA from the snippet library (Section 5).
6. **Validate** against Section 4 (voice rules), Section 5 (phrase library), and Section 6 (failure modes). Fix anything that does not pass.
7. **Optional**: run the style-guide voice checklist referenced in Section 7.

A complete first draft for a 10-minute Travis video, written from this playbook, should take about 60-90 minutes.

---

## 2. Pre-script checklist (answer before drafting)

Answer all of these in writing before you start the body. If you cannot answer one, the script will leak generic language.

1. **What is the one promise of this video?** State it in one sentence, ideally with a number ("how I found a $80,000/month product in 5 minutes", "7 secrets to going from 0 to $2.7M").
2. **Who is the viewer?** Pick one: `beginner_aspiring` (most Travis videos), `intermediate_seller`, `course_lead_aspirational`. The default in his catalog is beginner_aspiring (60%+ of advice and tutorial videos).
3. **What is the real result behind this video?** A dollar figure, a product, a student name, a count of years, a launch. If there is no real result, write the script as advice, not as a case study.
4. **What is the credibility line?** Pick from his stock: "I've done over $10 million in online sales", "I've been selling on Amazon for over 10 years", "I've launched over 500 products", "I created Performance Nut Butter", or a fresh result. Drop it inside the first 60 seconds.
5. **What is the enemy?** Travis almost always has one. Dropshipping gurus. Private label saturation. Jungle Scout overpaying. Corporate jobs. "Make money online" YouTubers. Name the enemy you are positioning against.
6. **What is the offer at the end?** Free 5-8 hour Amazon FBA course on YouTube, Passion Product Formula, Passion Product Accelerator, free e-commerce success pack, free call with the team, a downloadable. Pick one primary and one secondary.
7. **What are the 3-7 body beats?** Either steps, mistakes, secrets, costs, or pros vs cons. Write them as one-line titles before you write prose.
8. **What is the mid-roll micro-CTA?** "Smash the like button", "make sure to subscribe", or "stay till the end because I'll share [X]". Place it once around the 25-40% mark.
9. **Where does the personal anecdote land?** Travis works in at least one. Performance Nut Butter, corporate job at the meal-replacement company, Be Dancewear, his girlfriend Ariana, his student AJ, his student Anna, Carnivore Electrolytes.
10. **What is the signoff?** Default is "create a product, create freedom" or "enjoy the journey".

## 3. Templates by video_type

### 3A. `travis_solo_advice` (n=147, ~12 min median)

**When to use it**: The video argues a thesis, exposes a truth, or lists mistakes/secrets/reasons. There is no screen demo and no real-time tutorial. It teaches by opinion + framework, not by walk-through. This is the home of "the truth about Amazon FBA", "3 reasons private labeling is dead", "7 mindset hacks".

**Typical length**: 460-1043 seconds (p25-p75). Median 12 minutes. Aim for 8-17 minutes of finished video, roughly 1,400-3,000 words of script.

**Body skeleton**:

```
[HOOK: controversial_claim OR money_promise OR list_promise (numbered) - first 20 seconds]
[CREDIBILITY / NAME DROP: "my name is Travis" + dollar credential within first 60s]
[ENEMY CALL-OUT: who is lying, what old way is dead]
[STAKES: what you lose if you ignore this, or what you gain if you act]
[TEACH 1 / REASON 1 / MISTAKE 1 - with a personal example]
[TRANSITION: "and that brings us to" / "on top of that" / "the second reason"]
[TEACH 2 / REASON 2 / MISTAKE 2]
[MICRO CTA: "smash the like button" + tease something for later]
[TEACH 3 / REASON 3 / MISTAKE 3]
[continue numbered list - keep transitions clean]
[BIGGEST POINT RESERVED FOR #4 or end - "and the biggest one is..."]
[BRIDGE TO OFFER: "if you want to learn how to actually do this..."]
[MAIN CTA: free YouTube course OR Passion Product link]
[SIGNOFF: "create a product, create freedom" OR "enjoy the journey"]
```

**Verbatim hook examples that fit**:

- "I never lied to you on purpose but here's the truth about Amazon FBA that gurus won't tell you and recently I was exposed and called a liar..." (video_id: `GCdMTL7yhW4`, controversial_claim)
- "the old way of making money off Amazon is dead old school private labeling doesn't work anymore for three reasons China over saturation and basics..." (`s33yQs7MOBw`, controversial_claim)
- "these are the 10 mindset hacks i used to make over a million dollars last year and if you're suffering from analysis paralysis anxiety feeling lazy or just not hitting your goals this is the video for you..." (`P7o9wBXCUIY`, list_promise)
- "I'm sick of scamming YouTube gurus trying to trick people into private labeling on Amazon by talking about how easy it is or how much money they make..." (`dKGUUS0I7BE`, controversial_claim)
- "warning I'm about to piss off a lot of people sick of scamming YouTube channels trying to trick people into thinking that drop shipping is just a quick easy way to make money..." (`HTghzsltSNs`, controversial_claim)

**Signature transition phrases**:
- "on top of that"
- "and that brings us to"
- "the next thing is"
- "the second reason"
- "the first mistake"
- "now let me share with you"
- "so let's talk about"

**Ending move**: `course_link_pitch` (most common) or `free_resource_pitch`. Pair with "enjoy the journey" signoff or "create a product, create freedom". Add subscribe ask only if it fits naturally.

### 3B. `travis_solo_tutorial` (n=222, ~15 min median, frequently 30+ min)

**When to use it**: The video walks the viewer through a process. Steps, screen-share, software demos, A-to-Z guides. The viewer should be able to execute after watching.

**Typical length**: 554-1596 seconds (p25-p75). Median 15 minutes. Long-form tutorials run 25-50 minutes. Aim for 2,000-6,000 words.

**Body skeleton**:

```
[HOOK: tutorial_promise - "in this video I'm going to show you step by step..."]
[STAKES + CREDIBILITY: dollar figure + experience years in first 60s]
[ROAD MAP: "there's [N] steps" - state them all up front]
[STEP 1: title, what, why, exactly how, example]
[TRANSITION: "the next step is" / "step two" / "from there"]
[STEP 2: title, what, why, how, screen-share if applicable]
[MICRO CTA: "smash the like button" - place around step 2 or 3]
[STEP 3+ : same pattern, walk through every step]
[OPTIONAL LIVE EXAMPLE: use Performance Nut Butter, Carnivore Electrolytes, or a fresh product]
[WARNING / MISTAKE TO AVOID: insert near the end - "warning" voice tic]
[BRIDGE: "and if you want me to walk you through this even deeper..."]
[MAIN CTA: free 5-8 hour course OR Passion Product Formula link]
[SECONDARY CTA: "click up here for [related video]"]
[SIGNOFF]
```

### 3C. `student_case_study` (n=112, ~16 min median)

**When to use it**: The video centers on a real result. Either Travis's own ("I made $1.5M selling this product") or a student's (AJ, Anna, Ariana, James, Mina). The body walks through real numbers month by month or cost by cost. Revenue vs profit is always disambiguated.

**Typical length**: 747-1178 seconds (p25-p75). Median ~16 minutes. Aim for 1,800-3,500 words.

**Body skeleton**:

```
[HOOK: result_reveal with dollar number in first sentence]
[FRAMING: "and that number is revenue, not profit" - disambiguate immediately]
[CREDIBILITY + PROMISE: "we're going to share all the numbers including..."]
[INTRO THE PERSON: name, situation, why this matters to the viewer]
[STAKES TEASE: "and at the end I'll share one mistake that cost [him/her] $200,000 so stay till the end"]
[BACKSTORY: how they found the product / how they got started]
[STEP 1: product research / how they picked it]
[STEP 2: startup costs - break down by line item: design, packaging, inventory, shipping, Amazon fees]
[TRANSITION: "my next cost was" / "the next cost was"]
[STEP 3: launch numbers - month by month or first-launch reveal]
[MICRO CTA: "smash that like button" + "stay till the end for [the biggest mistake]"]
[STEP 4: ongoing revenue + profit reveal]
[THE BIGGEST MISTAKE / LESSON - reserved for end]
[BRIDGE TO OFFER: "if you want to do what [student] did..."]
[MAIN CTA: Passion Product Formula OR free YouTube course]
[SIGNOFF: "enjoy the journey"]
```

### 3D. `vs_comparison` (n=52, ~9 min median)

**When to use it**: Two named tools, platforms, or paths and the viewer needs to pick one. Shopify vs WooCommerce. Helium 10 vs Jungle Scout. Amazon vs Etsy vs Shopify. FBA vs FBM. Private label vs Passion Product.

**Typical length**: 315-906 seconds (p25-p75). Median 9 minutes. Aim for 1,200-2,200 words. These are tighter than advice or case studies.

**Body skeleton**:

```
[HOOK: question OR mystery OR tutorial_promise - tease the winner reveal]
[OPTIONAL DIRTY SECRET FRAMING: "there's some dirty little secrets about [A] and [B]..."]
[AFFILIATE / HOOKUP DISCLOSURE: "before we get started I have the hookup with [A] and [B]"]
[ROAD MAP: "we'll go pros of A, cons of A, pros of B, cons of B, and the winner at the end"]
[PROS of A: 3-5 points]
[CONS of A: 2-4 points]
[TRANSITION: "on the other hand" / "next up is" / "now let's talk about [B]"]
[PROS of B: 3-5 points]
[CONS of B: 2-4 points]
[MICRO CTA: "smash the like button" + "the winner is coming up"]
[OPTIONAL THIRD OPTION: only if relevant - "but with all that being said, there's actually a better option"]
[THE WINNER REVEAL: "the winner hands down is..."]
[PERSONAL RECOMMENDATION + WHY]
[BRIDGE TO OFFER: hookup link or free resource]
[MAIN CTA: free resource OR affiliate link with coupon code]
[SIGNOFF]
```

## 4. Universal Travis-voice rules

**DO:**

1. **Open with a number or a controversy in the first sentence.** Money, count of items, a "warning", or "the truth about". Do not warm up.
2. **Drop credibility within the first 60 seconds.** Pick one: "I've done over $10 million in online sales", "I've been selling on Amazon for over 10 years", "I've launched over 500 products". Do it once, not three times.
3. **Disambiguate revenue from profit every single time you say a money number.** "Revenue is not profit" appears 21 times in the corpus and on every single case study.
4. **Use "the truth is" as your reset.** It is the #2 voice tic across the whole catalog (137 occurrences). Use it to flag the honest take after a setup.
5. **Number everything.** Hooks, sections, tools, steps. "There's three steps", "seven secrets", "five mistakes". The number goes in the title and the opening line.
6. **Name a real product.** Performance Nut Butter, Be Dancewear, Carnivore Electrolytes, the coffin shelf, AJ's product, Anna's product. Abstract advice without a real product attached sounds generic and breaks voice.
7. **Tell on yourself.** Travis names his losses ("I lost over $20,000 due to manufacturing issues", "the mistake that cost me over a million dollars"). This is the credibility multiplier.
8. **Place the micro-CTA mid-video, not at the top.** "Smash the like button" lands at the 25-40% mark, almost never before the first teaching beat.
9. **End with "enjoy the journey" or "create a product, create freedom".** These are the two recurring signoffs.

**DON'T:**

1. **Don't use em-dashes.** Use periods, commas, parens. Spoken Travis does not have em-dash energy.
2. **Don't open with "Hey guys, welcome back to the channel."** He almost never does this. The hook is the open.
3. **Don't say "let's dive in" or "without further ado".** These are generic YouTube tropes. Travis says "let's get into the tactics" instead.
4. **Don't write "in this article" or "in this guide".** It is always "in this video" or "in this episode" (top opener: "in this video", 16-36 times depending on type).
5. **Don't soften with "potentially" or "may be able to".** Travis says it straight: "this works", "this is the best way", "this is dead".
6. **Don't pitch the paid program before earning the right to.** The course pitch lands after the teaching is done, in the last 15-20% of the video.
7. **Don't write corporate. No "leverage", no "synergy", no "robust", no "delve", no "navigate".** Travis says "smash", "boom", "huge opportunity", "the hookup", "freaking".
8. **Don't bury the dollar number.** If you have a result, it goes in the first sentence. Not paragraph two.
9. **Don't use AI hedging phrases.** "It is important to note", "as we have discussed", "in conclusion", "moreover", "furthermore" are banned.
10. **Don't end with "thanks for watching" alone.** Always pair the thanks with a CTA and the signature signoff.

## 6. Common failure modes ("not Travis" diagnostics)

1. **Cold corporate open.** Symptom: "Hello and welcome. Today we are discussing Amazon FBA strategies." Fix: lead with a dollar figure, a controversy, or a numbered promise. See Section 3 openers.
2. **Buried credibility.** Symptom: the dollar credential lands at minute 5 or never. Fix: get one credibility line into the first 60 seconds, then drop it.
3. **Revenue framed as profit.** Symptom: "I made $100,000" with no caveat. Fix: every dollar mention pairs with "revenue is not profit" or a profit number.
4. **Flat list with no enemy.** Symptom: pure information, no position, no enemy named. Fix: name dropshipping, white-label, the gurus, the old way, or saturated private label. Travis writes against something.
5. **AI tropes leaking in.** Symptom: "delve", "leverage", "robust", "navigate the complexities", em-dashes everywhere, "it is important to note". Fix: rewrite each one with a Section 5 snippet. Strip every em-dash.
6. **Generic "you" with no real product.** Symptom: advice with no Performance Nut Butter, AJ, Anna, Carnivore Electrolytes, or the coffin shelf as the example. Fix: graft a real product into every teaching beat.
7. **Pitch too early.** Symptom: course link mentioned in the first 90 seconds before any teaching has happened. Fix: tease the offer, but the main CTA pitch lives in the last 15-20%. Teach first, pitch after.
8. **Soft close.** Symptom: ends with "thanks for watching, bye." Fix: stack CTA + related-video plug + signature signoff ("enjoy the journey" or "create a product, create freedom").
9. **No micro-CTA.** Symptom: the script never asks for the like or a subscribe. Fix: insert "smash the like button" once at the 25-40% mark, paired with a tease for what is coming later in the video.
10. **Transitions that sound written, not spoken.** Symptom: "Subsequently", "Conversely", "In addition". Fix: use the Section 5 transition list. Travis transitions sound like a person talking.

## 7. Validation pass

Before handing off:

1. Read the script aloud at normal speed. If you hit an em-dash, kill it.
2. Compare every paragraph to the Section 4 DO/DON'T list.
3. Cross-check the voice tic count against the rough target (8-15 per 10 minutes).
4. Confirm the structure matches the chosen template skeleton in Section 3, in order, with no slots skipped.
5. Confirm the chosen ending move matches the template (course_link_pitch for tutorials/case studies/advice, free_resource_pitch for comparisons/listicles).
6. Run it through the style-guide voice checklist (companion doc in `/tmp/synthesis_outputs/`). If the checklist file is named differently in your synthesis run, search `/tmp/synthesis_outputs/` for `style-guide` or `voice-checklist`.

If all six pass, the draft is ready for Travis to review.
