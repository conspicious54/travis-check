# Sales Rep Video Playbook

A reference document with three sections:

1. **Typeform-driven sends.** Pre-call. Triggered by application answers.
2. **On-call objection follow-ups.** Sent during or right after the call. Triggered by what the lead says.
3. **AI-bucketed post-call sends.** Sent after the call. Triggered by the AI's analysis of the call recording.

Every video link is to content published within the last three years. Where a bucket has no strong existing video, the gap is marked with a concrete idea for what Travis should record.
