# Onboarding Sequence Flow

Internal-only rep reference for what to send a new student immediately after they pay, and how to book their orientation call.

This doc is technical and process-focused. Pricing, program details, and refund rules live in `payment-processing-faq.md` and `program-faq-sheet.md`. This one is just: how do we get a paying student from the moment of close into their orientation as fast as possible.

---

## A. WHICH TEMPLATE GOES TO WHICH PROGRAM

A student could have signed up for one of four programs:

1. **Accelerator** (the standard offer)
2. **Accelerator Advanced**
3. **Accelerator LITE**
4. **Incubator**

**For all three Accelerator variants, the template body is identical.** The only difference is the subject line:

| Program | Subject line treatment |
|---|---|
| **Accelerator** | Use the subject as written. |
| **Accelerator Advanced** | Append "Advanced" to the subject line. |
| **Accelerator LITE** | Append "Lite" to the subject line. |

**Incubator has its own dedicated template,** separate from the Accelerator templates. Don't reuse the Accelerator template for Incubator students. Pull the Incubator-specific one from the templates doc below.

## B. WHERE THE TEMPLATES LIVE

**Most up-to-date onboarding templates:** https://docs.google.com/document/u/2/d/1P90JKsuAtAY6HEFLEq7aUBy7YTz1brYolHZSPfjo55o/edit?tab=t.0

## C. WHEN TO SEND (CRITICAL)

**Send the template the moment the payment clears. Not later.**

Any gap between payment and the onboarding email is friction. The student is sitting in post-decision uncertainty, wondering if anything's actually happening. The faster the email lands, the smoother the handoff.

The rep workflow:
1. Payment clears (verified via the dashboard for whichever method they used).
2. Pull the correct template based on which program they bought.
3. Modify the subject line if Accelerator Advanced or LITE.
4. Send.

This happens before the rep moves on to the next call, the next deal, or anything else. Closing is not closed until the onboarding email is out the door.

---

## D. BOOK THE ORIENTATION CALL WHILE THE LEAD IS STILL ON THE PHONE (CRITICAL)

**If there's any time at the end of the close call, book the orientation call live with the lead while they're still on the line.** Walk them through picking a slot end to end.

Important: **the orientation call is not with the rep.** It's a handoff to the onboarding / coaching team. The rep's job is just to make sure the slot is locked in before the lead gets off the call, at a time the lead can actually make.

Why this matters:
- Orientations booked live on the close call **happen.** Orientations punted to "I'll book it later when I get a chance" tend to fall through, get rescheduled, or never happen at all.
- The faster the lead reaches orientation, the faster they're actually activated in the program. Orientation is the gate.
- If a lead falls off the orientation booking flow repeatedly, recovering them is a much bigger drag than the few minutes spent booking it live in the first place.

**Default behavior:** book the orientation live every time time allows. If the lead has to jump and can't book live, send the booking link immediately after and follow up actively until it's locked. Don't let a paid student float without an orientation slot on the calendar.

---

## E. CHECKLIST AT END OF EVERY CLOSE

Before the rep ends a call where a payment cleared:

- [ ] Payment confirmed in the relevant dashboard (Whop / Fanbasis / Elective / Clarity Pay / SFC).
- [ ] Correct onboarding template pulled from the live templates doc.
- [ ] Subject line modified if Accelerator Advanced or LITE.
- [ ] Onboarding email sent.
- [ ] Orientation call booked live with the lead, OR booking link sent and follow-up scheduled.

If any of those five aren't done, the close isn't done.
