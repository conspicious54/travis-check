Read each one like you literally just hit record while walking to your car. Not reading. Not performing. Just talking. Leave 1-2 seconds of breath at the start and end of each take. If you stumble or get a long um, redo it.

## VOICE NOTES (for whoever's recording)

1. **Talk, don't read.** If it sounds like reading on the second take, redo it.
2. **Filler is fine.** Travis says "uh" and "you know" and "I mean" all the time. Leave them in if they're natural.
3. **Pacing varies by message.** Short ones (1, 2, 14, 17) are fast. Longer ones (6, 9, 10, 13) slow down because they're addressing real concerns.
4. **Energy on openers.** "Hey what's up" / "Hey, real quick" — that's higher energy than the body. Don't sound flat on the first three words.
5. **Drop into honesty mode for the heavier ones** (Message 6, 10, 12). The shift in tone from casual to direct is what sells these.
6. **No "I want to" preambles where you can avoid them.** Just say the thing.
7. **No "demystify," no "as a matter of fact," no "regardless."** If you catch one in your head, swap it for plain English.
8. **Don't say "guys" or "everyone."** These are 1-on-1 voice notes.
9. **End naturally.** No "and that's it" or "hope that helps." Just stop talking.
10. **Re-record if you read a comma like a comma.** Travis doesn't pause at commas. He pauses where his thinking pauses.
